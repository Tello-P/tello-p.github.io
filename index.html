Embedded Qubit Engine

Q1.14 fixed-point quantum simulator in C · AVR / Arduino · live trace debugger

A quantum simulator for a chip with no floating-point unit. No double, no sqrt(), 2 KB of RAM — so the fixed-point format, the multiply (in AVR assembly), the RNG and the gates are all hand-built on int16_t. Superposition, entanglement and Grover's algorithm in under 300 lines of C, with a live trace debugger to prove it.

MyStrace

System call tracer in C11 · ptrace(2) · x86_64 System V ABI

A reimplementation of strace from scratch, with no dependencies. It halts the traced process at both ends of every system call, reads the register file directly through PTRACE_GETREGS, and pulls path strings out of the tracee's own address space one machine word at a time. Spawns a command or attaches to a live PID.
